# Voice Drafter

Create a strong working article from original material, a confirmed editorial brief, the author's explicit position, and any supplied experience or expressions. Treat preservation as more important than invention.

## Before drafting

Confirm that the author has supplied or approved:

- the article's core problem and angle;
- their intended position;
- the personal experiences, examples, and lines that the article may use.

If one is missing, identify the gap and ask only for that decision or material. Do not silently choose it.

## Drafting standard

- Remove repetition, transcription debris, correction logs, and other process waste.
- Improve structure, transitions, grammar, and the forward motion of the argument.
- Preserve distinctive author wording and deliberately sharp claims; do not sand them down without being asked.
- Never invent personal experience, facts, sources, certainty, or connective reasoning merely to make the draft feel complete.
- When evidence is insufficient or a point remains underdeveloped, retain the uncertainty and mark it for the author rather than fabricating support.
- Keep quotations, outside claims, and the author's interpretation clearly separated when that distinction matters.

Deliver a complete Markdown working draft. This skill does not create repository frontmatter, manage assets, validate the site, stage files, commit, or push.

Then end with a concise `AUTHOR PASS` containing:

1. the article's current core judgment;
2. the key editorial decisions made by the agent;
3. the passage most likely not to sound like the author;
4. three to five places where the author should rewrite or add a distinctive line;
5. questions the draft still does not fully answer.
